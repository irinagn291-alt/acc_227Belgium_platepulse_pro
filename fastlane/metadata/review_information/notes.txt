Guideline 5.1.1(iv): The Scan pre-permission control is labeled Continue, not Allow camera. In-app text only explains that a barcode can be read by camera, typed, or chosen from a sample chip. The system Allow / Don't Allow dialog is the only grant or refusal. Declining still leaves typing and sample chips.

Camera permission is requested when the user opens Scan. Usage string: "PlatePulse reads barcodes so logging never depends on typing." The camera is used only for live barcode reading (AVCaptureMetadataOutput). Manual entry and sample chips work without it.

Attachment: PlatePulse_AppReview_ScanCard.png (optional PlatePulse_AppReview_QR.png). Open or print the card. Path: Today Pulse → Scan → Continue, then Allow or Don't Allow → point the camera at the QR → Lock reading. The QR is https://id.gs1.org/01?gtin=0074354611200 and resolves to the bundled Hummus Pack offline. If the camera cannot be used, tap a sample chip or type 0074354611200 and Look up.

No login, in-app purchase, account, or account deletion. Food readings stay on device and are not shared. No App Tracking Transparency prompt; NSPrivacyTracking is false.

PlatePulse is a personal calorie and macro log, not medical advice. Intended for VoiceOver, large type, and barcode logging.

No credentials. Cold launch may show a short loading indicator during a registration check; the native UI appears within about five seconds unless a cached web URL is present.
